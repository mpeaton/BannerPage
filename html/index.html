The Mark

The AMDG logo is a 2×2 arrangement of high-tech blocks, each with a symbolic backdrop representing the company's core pillars. The animation highlights the internal dynamics of each quadrant.

A — Algorithms & AI

Semantic networks and knowledge graphs. The foundation of our work in state estimation, signal processing, and intelligent systems.

M — Medical Signal Processing

Animated EKG trace representing contributions to medical devices, robotics, and real-time physiological signal analysis.

D — Guidance & Direction

Compass rose — a quiet nod to the original hand-drawn “sun or son” symbol and the navigation/guidance theme running through AMDG.

G — Engineering & Circuits

PCB traces with green electricity animating along the paths — precision electronics, hardware, and systems integration.

About AMDG Technologies

AMDG Technologies, Inc. is a C-corp focused on STEM and work-study education programs, combined with defense-adjacent technical work, all filtered through a greater-good lens (Ad Majorem Dei Gloriam — “For the greater glory of God”).

Our technical roots are in signal processing, state estimation, sensor fusion, and medical device technologies. We believe advanced engineering and education should serve the common good.
